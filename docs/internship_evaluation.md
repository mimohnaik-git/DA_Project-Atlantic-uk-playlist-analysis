## Original Evaluator Feedback

The evaluator described the submitted version as a well-developed and highly interactive analytics project examining the structure and behaviour of the UK music market. The submission analysed more than 27,000 playlist entries and reported 359 artists and 803 “unique songs.” It covered artist concentration, collaborations, explicit versus clean content, release formats, track duration, market diversity, and UK/domestic versus international artist representation.

The evaluation highlighted approximate submitted results of 15.3% Top-5 concentration, a roughly 17% collaboration ratio, 32% explicit-content share and approximately 34% UK/domestic artist share. It recommended greater transparency around the Content Variety Index, HHI, diversity score, nationality classification and collaboration metrics.

This record preserves the substance of the original feedback. It does not imply that the evaluator reviewed the later remediated methodology. The original “803 unique songs” wording reflected the submitted title-count method; the current repository distinguishes 803 raw song titles, 798 normalized titles and 833 dataset-local recording proxies.

## Post-Evaluation Version

The current repository is a post-evaluation remediated version rather than an exact frozen copy of the internship submission. It preserves the business scope and dashboard while strengthening analytical units, snapshot reconstruction, recording identity, rank semantics, collaboration measures, artist weighting, diversity, HHI labeling, nationality documentation, validation, automated testing, CI, build tooling and causal-language discipline.

Atlantic Recording Corporation is the business scenario for the case study. It was not the employer or client, and the project was not an official Atlantic engagement.
